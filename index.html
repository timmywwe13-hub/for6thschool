ANOTHER LOBBY — LOCAL WIFI, OR AN ADDRESS SOMEBODY SENT
Alone: press PLAY - nothing else is needed.
Together, anywhere: the host sets the game online once - the two ONLINE boxes here, or the five steps in DEPLOY-ONLINE.md - and everybody opens the link. A Chromebook or a phone needs nothing installed, no address and no settings.
Together on the same wifi: one person runs PLAY-TOGETHER.cmd (or node lobby-server.js) and everybody opens the link it prints.
Already sent something? a link or a ws:// address goes in ANOTHER LOBBY below and presses CONNECT RELAY; a Supabase project sent as text (supabase:https://<project>.supabase.co|<key>) goes in the two ONLINE boxes.
What is real here: the friends list, the party and the formats are real game state, and the connections above are real transports — the ONLINE boxes are a free Supabase project, which needs nobody to run anything at all, “same browser” opens a live lobby any other window of this page joins, “connect” dials the lobby server you were served from, and ANOTHER LOBBY takes any websocket that speaks this JSON. And because the lobby relays between machines, friends on other devices are on the roster for real - they can be invited, or join the party the match is then built from.
